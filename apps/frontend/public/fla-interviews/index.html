FLA Interviews

Interview management by Future Leaders Foundation

FLA Interviews is an interview management platform developed by Future Leaders Foundation. It helps authorized staff organize candidate interviews, assign interviewers, publish appointments and communicate with selected candidates.

Application purpose

The platform allows candidates selected for the oral interview stage to view their assigned interviewer and choose or change an available appointment. Authorized administrators can manage assignments, availability, bookings and interview evaluations.

Candidate interviews and appointments

Candidates can select an available interview slot displayed in Morocco time. The platform sends booking confirmations, updates and reminders related to the interview.

Google Calendar and Google Meet

With authorization from a designated administrator, FLA Interviews creates and updates Google Calendar events, generates Google Meet links and manages candidate invitations when appointments change.

Limited Google data use

Google Calendar access is used only to provide the requested interview scheduling service. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Security and access control

Administrative features are protected by role-based access. Stored Google authorization tokens are encrypted and may be revoked by disconnecting Calendar or through the Google Account permissions page.
